# Changelog

## v2.0.0

Real-data, real-time trading lab. Additive: the v1 scheduling, revision, reconciliation and export surface and its
narrow GA promise are unchanged.

- `data/public`: 32 normalized, checksummed public data files (Elia Open Data and Energy-Charts, CC BY 4.0), including
  Elia's simulation of the new 2026 imbalance-price formula; `euroflex data fetch | list | verify | manifest`
- `realtime`: minute-level engine with quarter-hour settlement, explicit settlement operators for the current and
  new 2026 Belgian formulas (both reproduced from Elia's components), a publication model, and a perturbation audit
  against look-ahead; `euroflex rt validate-mechanisms | backtest`
- strategies: reactive thresholds, perfect-foresight oracle, forecast MPC, SCALES-L (mechanism-decoupled state
  forecasting), DQN baseline (`mps`), with walk-forward forecasters
- impact layer: own-price response operators, capacity-value curves, herding versus Nash equilibria
- aFRR reserve layer: capacity + energy + implicit balancing under one SOC with causal per-block allocation
- live paper trading on Elia near-real-time feeds (`euroflex live ...`), identical decisions to the backtest
- contract valuation: revenue scenarios with formula and saturation risk, toll / floor / partial toll / swap,
  attribution waterfall, portfolio risk (`euroflex value ...`)
- Germany market adapter (experimental): `da_only`, `da_plus_fcr`, `da_plus_afrr` (capacity-only)
- research reports in `reports/` regenerated by `scripts/research/*`, including a pre-registered prediction for the
  first weeks of the new Belgian formula
- fix: `PersistenceForecastProvider` no longer fails on the day after a DST change
- new optional extras `ml` (scikit-learn) and `rl` (PyTorch); `scipy` becomes a core dependency
