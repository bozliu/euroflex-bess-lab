# euroflex_bess_lab

**Version 2: a real-data, real-time trading lab.** The repository now contains the layer where battery revenue is
made in Belgium: minute-level implicit balancing settled per quarter-hour under Elia's current imbalance-price
formula and the new formula planned for 24 Nov 2026, aFRR reserve stacking, the price impact of large fleets and
herding, contract pricing (tolls, floors, swaps), a Germany adapter and a live paper-trading loop. All of it runs on
the committed public data set (`data/public`, Elia Open Data and Energy-Charts, CC BY 4.0) and every tradable strategy
passes a perturbation audit against look-ahead.

- [Real-time engine](realtime_engine.md) - mechanisms, information model, audit
- [Real-time strategies](realtime_strategies.md) - threshold, forecast MPC, SCALES-L, DQN
- [Impact and herding](impact_and_herding.md) - own-price impact, capacity-value curves, equilibria
- [Real-time reserves](realtime_reserves.md) - aFRR capacity + energy + implicit balancing

## v1 scheduling, revision and audit framework

`euroflex_bess_lab` is a public-core decision-support and operator-handoff framework for European BESS workflows. The current GA promise is intentionally narrow and centered on one canonical Belgium path, while the broader public surface now also includes a live-supported Netherlands workflow layer built around TenneT-backed ingestion, normalization, validation, reconciliation, and operator handoff.

`euroflex_bess_lab` is especially relevant for:

- BESS owners, operators, and optimizers
- flexibility aggregators and VPP teams
- trading, scheduling, and dispatch support desks
- diligence, benchmarking, and revenue-modeling teams
- teams connecting private forecast models to a deterministic market-rule layer

The repository is now centered on one narrow GA promise:

- Belgium
- portfolio / shared POI
- `workflow: schedule_revision`
- `revision.base_workflow: da_plus_afrr`
- forecast paths: `persistence`, `csv`
